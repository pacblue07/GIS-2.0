Participate

We invite you to participate in the Global Isolation Survey. There are several ways to get involved.

Contribute a building

The Global Isolation Survey is building an open database of seismic isolation projects around the world. We would like to know about your project. Please fill in one of the building data forms below and email it to us at info@globalisolationsurvey.org.

Building Data Form, simplePDF · the essentials: name, location, year, stories, isolation system, number of bearings Building Data Form, detailedPDF · adds public or private use, new or retrofit, owner, engineer, architect, design features and references

An online form is planned. A static site cannot receive submissions by itself, so this needs a form service; until then the PDF forms and email are used.

Corrections and photos

If you spot an error in the buildings database, or have photos of an isolated building you are willing to share, please email info@globalisolationsurvey.org with the building name and country.

Surveys

We are conducting periodic national and regional surveys to gain insight into the factors influencing the adoption and use of seismic isolation around the world. We will be undertaking surveys in Italy, Japan, New Zealand, South America, Turkey and the U.S. in the near future. If you are interested in participating, please contact us at info@globalisolationsurvey.org.
